Uzak Doğu'nun Ruhu — Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 31 July 2026 · Developer: KUT GAMES · Package: com.satirickarma63.udh

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Uzak Doğu'nun Ruhu ("Spirit of the Far East") developed by KUT GAMES. In short: this game does not collect, store or share any personal data.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect your name, email, location, phone number, contacts, photos, advertising ID, device identifiers or any other personal information. No account or sign-up is required.

2. Game saves

Your progress (currency, purchased characters and artifacts, achievements, settings) is stored only in local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted to any server and is not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. Internet access

The game is fully offline. It does not request the INTERNET permission and makes no network connections.

4. Ads and analytics

There are no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, etc.). There are no in-app purchases.

5. Permissions

  • VIBRATE — used solely for in-game haptic feedback and can be disabled in Settings. It does not read any data.

6. Children

The game is not directed to children; its target audience is 13 and older. Regardless, no data whatsoever is collected from any user, including children, so the app falls outside the scope of COPPA / GDPR-K data-collection requirements.

7. Sharing with third parties

No data is collected, therefore no data is shared with anyone.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
