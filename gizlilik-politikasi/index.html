Uzak Doğu'nun Ruhu — Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 14 August 2026 · Developer: SatiricKarma63 · Package: com.satirickarma63.udh

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Uzak Doğu'nun Ruhu ("Spirit of the Far East") developed by SatiricKarma63. In short: the developer does not collect, store or share any personal data. The game offers optional in-app purchases, which are handled entirely by Google Play; your payment details never reach the app.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect your name, email, location, phone number, contacts, photos, advertising ID, device identifiers or any other personal information. No account or sign-up is required. The developer operates no servers.

2. Game saves

Your progress (currency, unlocked characters and artifacts, achievements, settings) is stored only in local storage on your own device. It is never transmitted to any server and is not accessible to the developer. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

3. In-app purchases

The game offers optional purchasable items. These transactions are handled entirely by Google Play:

  • Your payment details never reach the app — card numbers, billing addresses and similar data are processed solely by Google. See Google's own policy at policies.google.com/privacy
  • The only thing the app learns is which items you own. This is kept in the save file on your device and used to restore your purchases.
  • Your purchase history is tied to your Google account, which is how your items return if you reinstall the app.
  • Purchases are optional. The entire game — every character and all 101 achievements — can be completed without paying.

4. Internet access

The game is played offline; no part of the gameplay requires a connection. The app goes online only for purchase transactions and for restoring items you have already bought. No other data is downloaded or uploaded.

5. Ads and analytics

There are no ads and no analytics or tracking SDKs (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Facebook SDK, etc.). The advertising ID (AD_ID) is neither requested nor read.

6. Permissions

  • VIBRATE — used solely for in-game haptic feedback and can be disabled in Settings. It does not read any data.
  • INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — used only for Google Play purchases.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING — required to offer purchases through Google Play.

7. Children

The game is not directed to children; its target audience is 13 and older. Regardless, no data whatsoever is collected from any user, including children, so the app falls outside the scope of COPPA / GDPR-K data-collection requirements.

8. Sharing with third parties

No data is collected by the developer, therefore no data is shared with anyone. For purchases, your counterparty is Google Play.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
